CAC REGISTRATION

What is CAC registration?

Registration with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) creates an official record for eligible businesses and organizations in Nigeria. The registration route depends on the structure of the business.

Common options

  • Business Name
  • Private Company Limited by Shares
  • Public Company Limited by Shares
  • Company Limited by Guarantee
  • Incorporated Trustees

What we can help you with

We can help you understand the registration route, prepare the required information, submit or support the application process, and explain queries or post-registration steps.

TRADEMARK REGISTRATION

Protect your brand identity

A trademark can help protect distinctive brand elements such as names, logos and other marks used in connection with goods or services. Registration is subject to the applicable examination and registration process.

Ask About Trademark Registration

Typical process

  1. SearchCheck the proposed mark and relevant class.
  2. ApplicationPrepare the applicant and mark details and submit the application.
  3. ExaminationThe application goes through the relevant examination process.
  4. Publication / oppositionWhere applicable, the mark proceeds through the publication and opposition stage.
  5. RegistrationIf the application completes the process successfully, registration documentation is issued.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to register a business?

The exact requirements depend on the registration type. Common information includes proposed names, applicant identification details, contact information, address and other details required for the selected structure.

Can you help me choose between a Business Name and a Limited Company?

Yes. We can explain the general differences and help you identify the information needed for the registration route you choose.

Can I register a trademark for my business name or logo?

A business name registration and a trademark are different forms of protection. We can help you understand the trademark application process and the relevant class for your goods or services.

How long does registration take?

Processing time can vary depending on the application, examination, queries, payment status and the relevant authority's processing time. We do not promise a fixed approval date.
